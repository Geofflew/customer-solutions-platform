# From customer records to useful follow-up

A customer team needs more than a score. It needs to understand what changed, why it changed, and who should act.

Product usage, meetings, notes, and contact records provide different kinds of evidence. Combining them into one account view helps only if the view preserves their meaning: disabled features should not look like failed adoption, a single ambiguous note should remain inspectable, and a name mentioned on a call should be checked against the customer roster.

Those account signals become useful when they support a decision. A weekly report can lead with changes and missing next steps. A saved customer group can show which accounts entered and left since review. A proposed assignment can preserve the exact change a person confirms.

The [repository overview](../README.md) explains these concepts and links the runnable implementation. Its records and displayed outcomes are synthetic; they are not production measurements or a record of customer incidents.
